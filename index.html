Bluebird Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026
Operator: Adrian Tanz · Melbourne, Australia
Contact: adrian@clubspearfish.com

Bluebird is a Victorian dive-day finder for iPhone. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used. Bluebird is designed to keep most data on your device.

1. Information we handle

On your device only

  • Favourite dive sites
  • Alert preferences (e.g. great dive day alerts on/off)
  • Optional local account details (name and email), if you choose to save them in Settings — stored on this phone only and not synced to our servers
  • Cached forecast data used by the app and Home Screen widgets
  • Local dive-day notification schedules (for example, a reminder when a favourite site is forecast at 9.0 or above)

Permissions you may grant

  • Notifications — if you enable great dive day alerts, iOS may ask for permission to show alerts, sounds, and banners. Notifications are scheduled and delivered by your iPhone; we do not run a Bluebird notification server that stores your device push token
  • Background App Refresh / background processing — used so Bluebird can periodically refresh forecasts and update scheduled alerts and widgets when the app is not open. Apple controls when background work runs

If you request a new site

  • Name, email, and the site details you enter are prepared in your device’s Mail app and sent to adrian@clubspearfish.com when you choose to send the message
  • We use that information only to review and respond to your site request

Forecast data

  • The app requests weather and marine forecast data from Open-Meteo for predefined Victorian dive sites. This is site-based forecast data, not your personal identity
  • Bluebird does not currently request access to your precise GPS location for account or advertising purposes

2. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information
  • We do not run advertising trackers or sell advertising profiles
  • We do not operate a Bluebird cloud account for syncing favourites across devices at this time
  • We do not send remote push notifications from our own servers. Alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device from forecast data already on the phone (cloud sync / server-side push may come later and would update this policy)

3. How information is used

  • To show dive-day scores, sites, and Home Screen widgets
  • To remember your favourites and alert preferences on your phone
  • To schedule local iPhone notifications when a favourite is forecast at 9.0 or above (including when Bluebird is closed), if you have alerts and notification permission enabled
  • To refresh forecasts in the background so those alerts and widgets stay reasonably up to date
  • To receive and action optional site requests you email to us

4. Third-party services

  • Open-Meteo — forecast requests for configured sites (including when the app refreshes in the background). See Open-Meteo’s own terms and privacy information for their processing of technical request data
  • Apple — app distribution, device storage, Mail, notifications, background processing, and widgets operate under Apple’s platform terms
  • Buy Me a Coffee — if you open the support link in the app, that site’s own privacy policy applies

5. Retention

  • On-device preferences, cached forecasts, and scheduled local notifications remain until you clear them, turn alerts off, delete the app, or reset the device
  • Site-request emails we receive are kept only as long as needed to assess the request and communicate with you, then deleted in the ordinary course of inbox management

6. Your choices

  • You can use Bluebird without saving a local account
  • You can clear a local account from Settings on your device
  • You can remove favourites and turn dive-day alerts off at any time
  • You can deny or later revoke notification permission in iOS Settings → Bluebird → Notifications
  • You can turn off Background App Refresh for Bluebird in iOS Settings (alerts and widgets may then only update when you open the app)
  • You can decline to send a site-request email after Mail opens
  • To ask us to delete a site-request email you sent, contact adrian@clubspearfish.com

7. Children

Bluebird is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. International note

The app is operated from Australia. If you email us a site request, that message is received in Australia. Forecast providers may process technical request data in other regions according to their own policies.

9. Changes

We may update this policy if Bluebird’s features change (for example, if we add cloud sync or server-side push notifications). The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

10. Contact
